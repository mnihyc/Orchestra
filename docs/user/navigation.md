# Navigation and tabs

Navigation follows the org selected in the rail. On a phone, choose the org at
the head of the Navigation sheet.

Lists and tables keep comfortable rows in either density. Compact changes
message spacing and avatar size.

## Tree and Recent

The two buttons in the Projects heading switch between Tree and Recent.
Tree keeps projects, folders, topics, threads, documents and chats in their places.
Recent lists the same permitted kinds across those places. **Opened** uses your
last visit. **Active** uses the last activity of threads and documents, including
agents' work. The groups are Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 days and Earlier.

Rows name their nearest parent. Its tooltip gives the full path. A place hint is
left out when it would be too short to read. Times stay whole: today uses now,
minutes or a clock; yesterday uses a clock, the preceding week a weekday, and
older items a date. A different year is included. The tooltip gives the exact time.
On a desktop the row's menu replaces its time on hover or selection. A phone
keeps both, letting the place give way first.
The name keeps its full width first. The place uses what remains; only a name
longer than the row's available space is shortened.

The beginning, narrowing words, Show choices, Hide done and Show archived apply
to Recent too. **All** beside the small Recent section opens the full list.
That small section is hidden while Projects shows Recent.

A row's menu has **Show in tree**. It switches back, opens the way to that row
and briefly marks it. The arrow keys and row menus work in both arrangements.
Each org's Navigation keeps its last arrangement and recent order on this device.

## Everything with more room

Choose **Open in Everything** from Projects' menu to open the wide table with
the same beginning, arrangement and recent order. Tree and Recent are the first
toolbar controls. Recent adds Where, Opened or Active, last activity or By, and
State. Columns, sorting and filters work in both arrangements.

Each arrangement keeps its own optional columns. Recent starts with its core
facts. Name takes its content width before Where uses the remaining space.
Where gives up the start of a long path, keeping the nearest places; its
tooltip gives the whole path. Extra columns scroll sideways inside the frame
with a visible scrollbar. Flat Recent rows have no tree indentation.

On a phone a Recent card shows name and state, nearest place and time, and By
when recorded. Additional labelled facts appear when you choose their columns.
When the time is aligned at the right edge, there is no separator after the place.

**Select** adds row ticks. **All** selects the rows currently shown. **Share**
opens the existing review for selected items. Rows that offer Share can be
selected; changing filters clears the selection.

The address carries the choices for links and Back. Opening a link saves its
choices as that list's previous view. Typed narrowing words are not remembered.
Rows you can no longer read disappear on the next read.

## Bars that slide out

On a desktop, a rail icon opens its view as a docked bar. An org icon opens that
org's Navigation. Press the front view's icon again to hide it; an org icon hides
Navigation when you are already at that org's home. Another icon switches views.
The title-bar toggle and shortcut also dock or hide the bar. The left-bar shortcut
is Ctrl+\ (Command+\ on Mac); the right-bar shortcut is Ctrl+Shift+.
(Command+Shift+. on Mac).

Point at a hidden bar's edge to peek at it over the page. The page keeps its width.

The temporary bar closes after the pointer leaves, when you open a row, or on
Escape. Focus returns to where it was. **Keep open as a bar** docks it again.
Reduced motion removes the slide. At medium widths, the right bar keeps its sheet.
Phones keep their Navigation and view sheets.

## Pinned tabs

Use **Pin** in a tab's menu or its right-click menu. Pinned tabs stand first in
their group and show a pin in place of the close button. Drag among the pins to
change their order. Dragging across the boundary pins or unpins a tab.

Close others and Close all leave pins open. Close a pinned tab with its own
**Close** menu item, or choose **Unpin** first. Pins stay through reload, Reset
layout and layout changes. Tab movement also works by keyboard: Space picks up,
arrows choose the destination, Enter drops and Escape cancels.
