# Accept an organization's terms

An organization can ask people to accept its terms before joining. The same terms apply to guest invitations to its places and group chats. Terms from the root organization appear first, followed by the nearer organizations' terms.

## Where

Open your invitation link. Read the scrollable **Terms** box and select **I accept the terms of …**. That one choice enables Join, or carries your acceptance into registration and sign-in with an identity provider. Signing in with an existing account returns you to the invitation.

To write terms, open the organization's **Settings** and find **Terms for people who join**. Edit that organization's own Markdown contribution and use Preview to read it with inherited terms. The field uses the usual settings permissions, locks and save controls. Start from [your organizations](/) and open the organization's settings.

## Example

Studio's root terms say to keep client material in Studio. Its Research organization adds an embargo date. A Research invitation shows both, in that order. Read both and tick the single checkbox, then Join. A member manager can see **Accepted the terms** and the recorded date on the member's row.

## What to expect

Acceptance applies to the exact text shown. If the terms change before joining, read the replacement and tick the checkbox again; typed registration fields stay in place. If a sign-in round trip ends because terms changed, return to the invitation and start again after reviewing them.

An organization without terms has no checkbox. Existing members are not asked to accept every later edit. To tell them about a change, use [messages from admins](messages-from-admins.md). System invitations have no organization terms.
