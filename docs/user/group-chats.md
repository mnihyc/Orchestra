# Give a group chat a home

A group chat has a name, a home, visibility and managers. Its home can be an organization, project, topic or thread. The place's own chat remains its public conversation; a separately created group can be private or public.

## Where

From your organization's Messages page, use New message to choose people. One recipient starts a direct conversation; turn on Group to create a group with that person instead. Several recipients turn on Group automatically. Choose an optional Name, Home and Visibility. Home starts at the place that opened the dialog. A group appears under its readable home in navigation, in Messages grouped by home and in [Everything](/everything). Open its header menu or Share to inspect members and management.

**Public** lets people with the home's Chat View discover the group and join themselves. **Private** uses a manager's invitation or an approved join request. A private group's name is not exposed to a nonmember through the public list.

Message… opens the conversation without joining. Join is shown when you may join yourself. Share opens the group's actual roster, manager roles, visibility and Home control. A manager can move it to another organization, project, topic or thread within the same root organization; the group then appears under its new home.

## Example

Create a private group called Launch review under the Release topic. Add the reviewers, then make another reviewer a manager before handing over. When wider participation is appropriate, a manager can change visibility to Public. People who can view chats at that home can then find and join it; an unrelated outsider does not gain access.

## What to expect

Managers can add and remove members, decide requests, change the name, home and visibility, and manage agent posting. The last manager must hand management to someone else before giving it up. Home administrators retain the place's normal moderation and administrative reach.

Moving a group requires the offered rights at its current and new homes. Sharing is still participation in that group: read the native access choices before adding people. See [link sharing](link-sharing.md) for a copied link's audience, and [resource groups](resource-groups.md) for organizing resource lists.
