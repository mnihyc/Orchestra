# People and mentions

## Open a person

Point at a person or focus their chip to see quick actions. Click the chip or press Enter to keep their profile open. On a touch screen, tap the chip to open the profile and its quick actions. Message opens a conversation when messaging is available. Your own chip opens your profile without offering Notify, Give access or Message; inserting your own mention opens no notification prompt.

## Notify and share

Notify sends an Inbox item with an optional note and a link to the work. The recipient must be able to read it. Give access offers the levels you may share; choose Notify them if the grant should include a notice. The grant and notice are one action.

## Mention in a document

Type @ and choose a person. Readers appear first; people without access are marked No access. Choose More people to load the next page, or First people to return to the beginning. Only the current page is shown. A row selects the person; hover, press Right Arrow, or tap Inspect to look at them first. Escape returns to your text. After inserting a mention, choose Notify for a reader. For someone without access, choose Give view access, Give access and notify, or Leave. Mentioning someone in a document or comment does not notify them by itself. You choose Notify.

Chat mentions remain part of the message you send. Changing access can remove a notice's private detail from the recipient's Inbox.

## Notification progress

A discussion with many recipients saves immediately and sends its notices in the background. Comments shows Notifying people and the checked count. Stop notifications stops the remaining work; notices already delivered stay in the recipient's Inbox. Progress resumes after a server restart.

## Read older discussions and comments

Comments initially shows up to 50 recent discussions and the latest 50 replies in each. Choose **Older discussions** or **Older comments** to continue, or **Latest discussions** / **Latest comments** to return. Each action replaces the current page. The order selector sorts discussions within the displayed page. Writing a reply returns that discussion to its latest comments; saved drafts stay on this device when you change pages. An author may resolve their discussion even when their earlier reply is on another page. Document exports that include comments keep the full history.
