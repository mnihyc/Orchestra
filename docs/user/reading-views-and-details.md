# Reading views and details

## Opening a screen

Open a thread or a resource by its name. Its first screen shows the information you may read.
A closed navigation view or Documents section loads its contents when you open it.
A name already shown elsewhere does not grant access. A permission change can make an open item unavailable.

Loading shows skeleton rows. A failed read keeps its error and the offered next action.
An unknown amount stays unknown; it is not a zero.

## Views and navigation

Tabs and view controls use the same names on a phone and a desktop.
Every tab shows its name, including the active tab. A long name truncates when the strip is narrow.
A phone keeps the current tab visible. Find tab locates another open view.
Two views with the same name use their kind or readable path to tell them apart.
A remaining repeated view gets its own view number. The item's own name stays unchanged.
Personal spaces and chats include their purpose in page titles and tabs.
While a page reloads, its tab keeps its name.

**Resource navigation** opens the list of resource kinds. **Resources** opens the resource overview.
**Place documents** follows the current place or the place fixed to that view.
**System templates** is the Management inventory. **Provider usage** is the provider's report;
**Consumption**, Performance and Requests select the report's contents.

## Cards and menus

Rest the pointer on a person's chip, focus it, or press it to read their card.
Resource and reference chips open their own details in the same way.
Long names are shortened in compact rows. Hover or focus reveals the whole name.

Cards, help, menus, select lists and date pickers stay inside the screen.
A long card or list scrolls inside its available space. A phone uses the same small help card.
Press Escape to close the top layer. Closing a kept card returns to its control.

A row's menu stays beside its first name line, even when the row has more facts below it.
Right-click and the keyboard menu key open the same actions where offered.
An unavailable action keeps its reason; opening a card does not change what you may do.

## Values and settings

A setting keeps its value and source beside its name. Its menu and question mark belong to that name.
Compact preferences keep their guidance in a tooltip. Hover or focus the control to read it.
Open the question mark for more help and the linked article.

A bell shows the served notification level. Chat's Off is separate from a place's Off.
A change is pending until its answer arrives. A refused discussion change keeps the served level
and opens its error beside the bell. Escape closes that error and returns focus to the bell.

## Reading documents and files

Document titles, headings and file contents are the author's data. Repeated words in them are kept.
The document's attachment button names the document it adds files to.
A task's checkbox names its task. Choose the code language from its list. Copy takes the whole block.
Audio and video from an external address load only after you choose Play.

File names, addresses, image references and service names keep their exact spelling.
Their technical values can be copied as shown. A display keeps its name and menu together;
its helper command remains beside its own Copy action.

## Finding names and commands

Press `Ctrl+K` (`Command+K` on macOS) to open Find anything.
Choose **All** to remove a mode sign and find names, commands, settings and help together.
A mode button writes its sign in the field. You can type the same sign yourself.
Use **Conversations** or `/` to find what was said.
