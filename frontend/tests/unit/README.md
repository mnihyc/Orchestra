Behavior that needs no browser: the API client (a token renewed once, errors as typed values, the
header of the cookie requests, a wait that ends on silence alone), the layout tree of the workbench
and what a device keeps of it, the live connection (gap repair, duplicates, reconnecting), the tree
of an org as the left bar lays it out (folders to any depth, what waits on a closed row, rows that are
not drawn again when nothing in them changed), where a project, a folder or a topic can be moved to,
the cards of a project's folders, the two catalogues held to the same keys, and times, sizes,
avatar text and shortcuts in both languages. What a page keeps of a chat is held here too: rows that
replace only what is older, a window of the past that is not broken by what is new, who is typing,
what is unread, a load that says the chat can no longer be read, the plain parts of a message (its
text, mentions and the Markdown it may hold), where a forward can go, and what quoting items of a
thread into its chat leaves for the composer. What the resources screens say once for all is held here too:
the acts of a row and the menu and keys they make, the list that the way back returns to as it was left, the
words every hint has in both languages, and when a machine is to be enrolled again.
The finder's ranking over settings, commands, rows of a page and help, the
signs that keep it to one kind of answer, how its groups are put in order and what a name leads to (a folder with the way
to it, a person by address); what a hit of a search is (the match marked where the server counted it, in characters, where it
opens, the address that holds the page's state, the days of a span in the reader's zone); the user guide as data, the links
between its articles, and what holds every hint to a text in both languages and to an article that is there; and the list of
keys, which is made from the keys that are bound. And the model of documents: a page of blocks to and from the editor's state, the batch of
operations a change makes, what the keys do to a page, other people's rows laid over it, and Markdown read and pasted.

The left bar's own rules are held here as well: a topic that is done in its place, what is unread
and what a row that hides it carries (a dot, and a number only for decisions and mentions), chats in
the tree, where the tree begins and what is folded away, what the bar keeps for a person and what a
device keeps, the marks of the orgs in the rail, and the rows of the Resources view.

The decisions of the settings screens that need no browser: where a value in force comes from and
what a lock at the place itself or above it means, the control a key's schema asks for, what the form
of a kind of channel sends, the code to scan for an authenticator app, and the small rules of the
personal pages.

Add checks with product behavior; do not assert file layout, import choices, component shape or
other implementation details.

What the giving screens say once for all is held here too: what a reader may give and the words that say why the
rest is not, which choice a role is, what sharing with agents means by area and how far a share goes, what a place's
share covers and leaves out, and what travels with a choice.

What is ticked in the tree of a place and what request that comes to (everything as one word, whole places named, the documents and
chats of a place that is only partly in, rows that cannot be taken away read as no part of it), the request an archive is asked with
and how its plan reads against the limits of a place, who may take a thread, a chat and a document away, and what a menu says of it.

What a thread would be bound to, from what the server says and what the person chose: what the defaults of a place settle, what the inline
starter asks for and a dialog suggests, a default that cannot be used and is not swapped for another, a harness that has not reported and is
waited for, which arrangement is ticked and which one the server would take, a way that cannot be had left closed with its reason, the
environment (the place's own, a new one, one made a moment ago), and what is kept as the default of a place.
