# Side threads

Built: asking on the side. A side thread is the same agent with the same files as its thread, started from a
message (from the menu of the item, which takes the history up to it) or from the menu or the box of the thread,
with one line. It is shown under its recorded source turn: a short exchange
of its own with its own box, folded or closed from there, or kept as a thread of the topic (the wizard of a fork,
for what it is). A thread may have so many open (the system says how many); at the limit the answer is in place,
the open ones each with a way to close them. A chip in the header of the thread counts the open ones and lists them.
Side threads are not in the tree of places and not in the lists of threads: they are the thread's own.
The header, message actions, composer menu and dialog use the thread's verified side mode and reason,
never policy permission alone or an inferred capability. Unverified modes leave the entry unavailable
with a short localized reason; the question dialog retains the harness's full reported explanation.
The dialog and lane name the enforced mechanism: read-only files, denied permission requests with only
reading admitted, or supplied context without tools. An unsupported aside keeps its reason and Fork to
beside the header action. The dialog uses an already loaded thread; only an absent one is read.
Escape keeps the current question and its retry identity. A lost answer freezes that exact attempt for
Try again, including after closing, navigation or a consumer remount; simultaneous consumers share
one in-flight request at the same thread/history boundary. Retry keeps its progress and focus. Only
confirmed acceptance clears unchanged submitted words, preserving newer words and attachments in the
main composer. Enter sends outside IME, Shift+Enter adds a line, and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter also sends.
A deliberate different aside starts a fresh form once any unanswered act at that boundary is resolved.
Closed dialogs read nothing, and account changes forget retained drafts and acts; a late answer cannot
clear the next person's work. A cached entrance name is never shown before the current thread is readable.

The shared conversation lane renders both sub-agents and asides with the same compact item displays.
An open aside mounts its own ordinary feed and draft; a folded, hidden or closed aside mounts neither.
Its inline composer sends on Enter outside IME, preserves Shift+Enter, and keeps its queue and Stop separate
from the main conversation. A viewer gets the conversation and a watch notice. Close is for its maker or
a parent manager; Keep follows the native fork permission. Open in panel moves the same conversation and
draft into the Conversation container. A lane and panel sharing a draft use one debounce owner and ordered
writes; an older save cannot follow the clear for a sent message, and queued work is fenced on account change.
The open list merges its one explicit read with parent start/close markers, without count-driven refetching.
Component, row and browser sources cover those states; the joined native browser case requires a fresh
scripted thread with a verified reading mode and integration-runner evidence, separate from the HTTP double.

- `ask.ts`: Asking on the side: the entry every screen calls, and the one request behind it.
- `availability.ts`: Side-thread availability comes from the thread's verified reading mode and current authority.
- `AskOnSide.tsx`: The one line that starts a side thread, and what is said when a thread has as many open as it may.
- `SideLane.tsx`: A side thread beside the conversation: its own short exchange with the same agent, named after its thread, kept by a fork or closed.
- `SideChips.tsx`: The open side threads of a thread, as one chip in its header, with what each is and a way to close it.
